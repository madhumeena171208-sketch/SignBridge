# 🌉 SignBridge
> *Every sign deserves to be understood.* Proposed real-time Indian Sign Language (ISL) to text experience for video calls. Built for **Kritical Hacks 2026**.

---

## 💡 The Problem
* **330M+** hard-of-hearing people worldwide (**63M+** in India).
* **Severe Interpreter Shortage:** Only ~300 certified ISL interpreters in India.
* **Communication Barrier:** Most hearing people do not know sign language, and standard video-call applications lack built-in ISL translation support[cite: 1].

---

## 🚀 Proposed Experience & Architecture
SignBridge translates video feed landmarks into readable captions live during a video call, enhanced with contextual search powered by the **TinyFish API**.
